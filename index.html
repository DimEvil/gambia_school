Jarra West Education Initiative

Empowering Children in Sare Saidy Village

Located in the Jarra West District of the Lower River Region (just 9.2 km from regional hub Mansa Konko), Sare Saidy is a vibrant rural farming community. Until now, young children had to walk long miles under intense sun to reach primary schools in neighboring settlements.

Sare Saidy Lower Basic Cycle School (LBCS) is designed to change that forever. Built in close partnership with village elders, community organizers, and local master builders, the school will feature 4 weather-proof classroom blocks, solar ceiling fans, and a clean drinking water borehole.

100% Direct Non-Profit Guarantee

Every euro goes straight to physical construction materials (masonry cement, timber, corrugated roofing sheets) and fair local wages for Gambian builders. 0% administrative overhead.
